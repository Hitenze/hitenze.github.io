Interactive Playgrounds

  • GP ExplorerVisualize GP priors, posteriors, and hyperparameter sampling with MCMC and HMC.
  • NN PlaygroundExperiment with network width, regularization, dropout, and optimizers in a simple regression demo.
  • CNN PlaygroundInspect feature maps, compare small CNN variants, and test digit predictions with sample or hand-drawn inputs.
  • RNN PlaygroundCompare RNN and LSTM sequence models, inspect hidden dynamics, and watch forecasting behavior across datasets.
  • GCN PlaygroundExplore node classification, oversmoothing, residual connections, and label scarcity on small graph datasets.
  • Transformer PlaygroundInspect causal self-attention, compare head and depth settings, and interact with a tiny language model.

Online Toolbox

A collection of practical web utilities for everyday tasks.

  • Photo MosaicBlur selected areas of an image to protect privacy.
  • Text FormatterClean whitespace, line breaks, and adjust casing.
  • URL to QRConvert URLs to scannable QR codes.
  • Exam TimerLarge-screen timer for exams or talks.
  • JSON FormatterValidate, pretty-print, and minify JSON.
  • Base64 Encoder / DecoderConvert text and files to/from Base64.
  • Timestamp ConverterConvert between Unix timestamp and datetime.
  • URL Encode / DecodeEncode/decode full URLs or URL components.
  • Password GeneratorCreate secure, random passwords.
