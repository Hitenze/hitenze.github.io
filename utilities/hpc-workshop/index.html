HPC Workshop

A practical 90-minute introduction to research computing

Learn how clusters are organized, request resources with Slurm, and run a small Python job without treating the login node as a workstation.

Who Should Attend

Graduate Students

Master's and PhD students looking to accelerate their research

Researchers New to HPC

No prior HPC experience needed - we'll start from the basics

Anyone Curious About HPC

Learn when cluster resources are a practical fit for your workload

No prior HPC experience is required. You will still need a cluster account, an SSH client, and your institution's login and MFA details for the hands-on steps.

Learning Outcomes

Grasp the basics of parallel computing

Connect securely and translate a workload into site-appropriate time, memory, CPU-slot, and GPU requests

Write and submit a minimal Slurm job script

Validate scripts, inspect job evidence, and keep Python environments reproducible
